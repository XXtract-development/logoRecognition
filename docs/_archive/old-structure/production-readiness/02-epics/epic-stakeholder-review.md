## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Logo Recognition System consists of **8 interconnected epics** spanning **20 weeks** of development with a total scope of **280 story points**. The system will deliver intelligent logo recognition with 99% accuracy, requiring only 5-10 training samples per logo.

### Investment Overview
- **Total Duration:** 20 weeks (5 months)
- **Total Effort:** 280 story points
- **Team Size Required:** 5-7 FTEs + 1 UX Designer (part-time)
- **Estimated Budget:** €200-300k (excluding infrastructure)
- **Expected ROI:** 342% within 12 months
