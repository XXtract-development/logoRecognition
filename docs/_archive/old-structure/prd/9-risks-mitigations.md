# 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Accuracy <99% | High | Medium | Extensive validation, threshold tuning |
| Slow training | Medium | Low | GPU acceleration, caching |
| Scale limitations | High | Low | Horizontal scaling design |
| User adoption | Medium | Medium | Intuitive UX, training materials |
| Data privacy | High | Low | Encryption, GDPR compliance |
